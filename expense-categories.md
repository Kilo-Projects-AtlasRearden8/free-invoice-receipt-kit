# Expense Categories

Printable reference, no account needed. Credit: Asdale Dynamics, LLC.

Use these categories when logging receipts, invoicing, or preparing your books. Adjust to fit your business.

| Category | Typical examples |
|----------|------------------|
| Advertising & marketing | Ads, social media boosts, business cards, flyers, KDP ads |
| Bank & payment fees | Bank fees, PayPal/Stripe fees, currency conversion |
| Books & education | Reference books, courses, AI tool subscriptions for learning |
| Car & mileage | Business mileage, parking, tolls |
| Home office | Desk, chair, internet portion, utilities portion, office supplies |
| Meals (business) | Client meals, travel meals subject to tax limits |
| Professional services | Accountant, tax preparer, lawyer, designer, editor |
| Software & tools | SaaS subscriptions, domain, hosting, AI writing/coding tools |
| Supplies | Paper, ink, pens, shipping materials, small equipment |
| Travel | Airfare, lodging, rideshare for business trips |
| Other | Any expense not listed above &mdash; describe it |
